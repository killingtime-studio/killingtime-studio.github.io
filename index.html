Killing Time: Fact or Fiction

Real or made up? You decide.

Killing Time is an original storytelling channel on YouTube, inspired by classic "fact or fiction" TV shows. Every episode tells several strange stories. Some are real cases from history; some we made up. Viewers guess which is which, and the answer and sources are revealed at the end of the same video.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@killingtime_StoryTelling
