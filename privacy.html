Privacy Policy

Killing Time Uploader · Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. What this app is

Killing Time Uploader is a private tool made and used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Killing Time: Fact or Fiction" to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

2. YouTube API Services

Killing Time Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access

Only with the permission the channel owner grants through Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0), the app accesses:

  • The owner's own channel information and list of uploaded videos
  • Video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and publish schedules that the owner uploads to the owner's own channel
  • YouTube Analytics reports (such as views and watch time) for the owner's own channel

The app does not access other users' data or other channels.

4. How we use it

This information is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to review the channel's performance so future episodes can be improved.

5. Storage and sharing

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's personal computer and is never shared or committed to any code repository. Performance notes are kept as local files for the owner's own use. We do not sell any information or share it with third parties. The app does not use cookies and does not collect information from visitors to this website.

6. Deleting your data and revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, or upon request, the stored token and any data obtained from the YouTube API Services are deleted from the owner's computer within 7 days. Data obtained from the YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

7. Contact

Contact us through the YouTube channel @killingtime_StoryTelling.
